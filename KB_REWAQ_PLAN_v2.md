## 🎯 PILLAR 5 — ACQUISITION CHANNELS (how we get leads)

1. **Google Maps no-website scrape** — search "restaurant/salon/retail in [area], Kuwait", filter listings with NO website button. 15–40 qualified leads per scan. (Free manual, or Outscraper/GrapeLeads/B2BLeadFinder for scale — 7-day trials, no card.)

**Lead workflow:** scrape → enrich (name, phone, area, lang) → audit sample → pitch → CRM track.

## 💳 PILLAR 6 — OPERATIONS (payments, legal, tools)

### ⚠️ CRITICAL CORRECTION from old plan
Old plan assumed KNET. **KNET is for Kuwaiti MERCHANTS accepting local debit cards** — we (India seller) CANNOT use it. We receive payment as a foreign freelancer.

### How WE get paid (India → Kuwait)
- **Wise / Payoneer / Skydo / Xflow** — receive USD/KWD-equivalent, lands in INR bank.
- **PayPal** works but higher fees + limited IN features.
- Invoice in **KWD or USD**, accept via Wise/Payoneer link.
- Note: >₹20L/yr → 18% GST + FIRC for forex proof.

### Delivery stack (all local/offline, no cloud APIs)
- Site: HTML/CSS/JS templates + Tailwind, deploy free (GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel — no card)
- Design: Canva + Figma + our poster-generator.html (already built)
- Ads: client gives us Meta Business Manager access (we run, they pay ad spend)
- CRM: `crm.json` + simple Node script (build this time — old plan never did)

### Legal
- India entity not strictly required to start; operate as freelancer/sole prop.
- Kuwait has no tax treaty issue for remote B2B services; we don't need Kuwaiti license to serve them remotely.
