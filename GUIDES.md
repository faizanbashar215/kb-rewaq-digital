Isse clients ko lagta hai aap 24/7 available ho. Setup 5 min ka hai.

- Play Store / App Store se **WhatsApp Business** (green briefcase icon) install karo
- Apna number **+965 50703252** verify karo
- Profile mein: Business name = **KB Rewaq Digital**, category = Marketing/Web

## Step 2 — Away / Greeting Message (auto)
WhatsApp Business → ⚙️ Settings → Business tools → **Away message**
- ON karo

## Step 4 — Catalog (optional, for real estate/grocery)
Settings → Business tools → **Catalog** → Add products (posters you make).
Client direct WhatsApp par aapka catalog dekh sakta hai.

# 🍔 Talabat Listing Setup — Guide for Restaurants/Cafes

Talabat = Kuwait ka sabse bada food delivery app. Restaurant clients ke liye
listing setup karke aap unhe ₹20 KWD extra charge kar sakte ho.

## Client ko bola do:
1. **Register:** talabat.com/business → "Become a Partner" → Kuwait
2. Documents chahiye: Civil ID, restaurant license, menu, bank IBAN
3. **Menu upload:** photos + price + Arabic/English names
4. **Branding:** logo + cover photo (hum banayenge — poster tool se)
5. **Offers:** "20% off first order" lagao → zyada orders

## Aapka kaam (KB Rewaq Digital):
- Cover photo + logo design (poster generator se) → client ko do
- Menu Excel → Talabat format mein arrange
- Listing description likho (EN + AR)
- ₹20 KWD charge karo is setup ka

## Upsell:
"Listing ban gayi, ab Instagram pe bhi 30 posts chahiye?" → Monthly plan ₹55 KWD
